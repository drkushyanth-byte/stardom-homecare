# CareConnect Home Care — delivery outcomes

- Build a welcoming homepage that clearly explains CareConnect Home Care and its purpose: helping people who need a home worker contact the team and request worker details.
- Add a services section describing common home-care support available, including companionship, daily-living support, recovery support, and household help.
- Add a simple overview of how families can request and receive worker details.
- Add trust-building content covering care standards, reliability, respectful matching, and family-focused support.
- Add an easy inquiry form for visitors to request details about available home workers, with accessible labels, required fields, validation, and a clear success/next-step message.
- Clearly display phone, email, and service-area contact information, with a visible note that demo contact details must be replaced before launch.
- Make the layout responsive and accessible for phones, tablets, and desktops, with keyboard-friendly navigation, readable contrast, focus states, and a mobile menu.
